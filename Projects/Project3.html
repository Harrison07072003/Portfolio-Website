Movie Description

After all of the Angels are defeated by NERV, Commander Gendo Ikari and SEELE end their turbulent alliance. Gendo intends to begin Third Impact on his own terms by merging Rei with Lilith. In order to stop him and start their own scenario for Instrumentality, SEELE tries to hack into the Magi computer. After Ritsuko defeats their hacking attempt, SEELE tricks the Japanese Strategic Self Defense Forces into ruthlessly attacking NERV Headquarters. Different Forces try to unlock the power of human Instrumentality and at the center of it all lies Unit 01 and its pilot: Shinji Ikari

Movie Review

This movie is definently one that has changed how I see and view the world. This is the finale to the TV series of the same name and it as able to capture the climatic and cinematic end that the series had been building up to. In partciular the characters are all well characterized and all have differenent qualities and flaws that make them feel so human. These qualities resonated with me throughout the show and I loved how we see the finale to all the individual character stories through this move to understand what this character represnets in the grand message on the film. The imagery in this movie is very interesting to with it containing many great shots and frames that leave a promiment impact when watching the film. The most titular scene of the movie is the third impact which is an event which the series has built anticipation towards. We get to see the scene in its full impact and we see the devastation from the event. Overall, I would say this movie is worth watching especially after the TV series as it provides a nice finale to the whole thing and allows for you experaince a film that is truly unforgettable.
