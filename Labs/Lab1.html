Information Technology

Information Technology (ITEC) is the multidisciplinary study, design, development, implementation, support of managament of computer-based information systems.

At GGC, we offer Bachelor of Science degree in information technology

Concentrations

Information Technology Deparment provides 5 concentrations:

Digital Media
This concentration is ideally suited for jobs involving digital media.

Enterprise Systems
This concentration is for students interested in information technology positions in business.

Software Development
This concentration is ideally suited for jobs in software development.

Systems and Security
This concentration is ideally suited for jobs involving system networks and security of those sytems.

Data Science and Analytics
This concentration is a data-driven program applying mathematics, computing and domain knowledge.
